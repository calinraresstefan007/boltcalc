Bolt Calc Privacy Policy
========================

Last updated: 2026-11-16

This app ("Bolt Calc") helps Bolt drivers evaluate ride offers by calculating profitability based on fuel costs, commission, and bonuses.

DATA COLLECTION AND USE
-----------------------

This app DOES NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data with external servers. All data processing happens locally on your device.

What data is processed locally:
- Bolt ride offer text (read from screen via Accessibility Service)
- Your configured settings (fuel consumption, fuel price, commission rate, etc.)
- Bonus campaign data (targets, progress, amounts)
- Last read offer text (for calibration/debugging)

All data is stored in Android SharedPreferences on your device only. No analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party services.

PERMISSIONS
-----------

1. Accessibility Service (android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)
   - Purpose: Read ride offer text from the Bolt Driver app screen
   - Scope: Limited to package "ee.mtakso.driver" (Bolt Driver app only)
   - Data accessed: Text content of ride offer screens (price, distance, time)
   - Data NOT accessed: Personal messages, contacts, location, other apps

2. Display Over Other Apps (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)
   - Purpose: Show profitability overlay on top of Bolt Driver app
   - Data displayed: Calculated profit, earnings per km, verdict (Excellent/Good/Reject)

DATA RETENTION
--------------

- Settings: Persisted until you change or uninstall the app
- Calibration text: Last read offer text, overwritten on each new offer
- Bonus progress: Persisted until you change or uninstall the app

YOUR RIGHTS
-----------

- You can disable the Accessibility Service anytime in Settings → Accessibility
- You can revoke "Display over other apps" permission in Settings → Apps → Bolt Calc
- You can clear all data by uninstalling the app
- No data is sent to any server, so there's nothing to delete from our side
